About us :

Over the past years, we have proudly tried to be a designer, manufacturer and supplier of industrial equipment alongside the oil and gas, petrochemical, water and sewage, steel, aluminum, copper, etc. industries. Therefore, due to the wideness of the needs of the mentioned industries, as well as taking steps towards the realization of the national mission and localizing the parts, relying on modern technical knowledge and engineering experiences, and the manufacture and knowledge of rotating and fixed parts and equipment such as pumps, turbines, compressors, etc. .. We decided to establish Fardad Machine Company to continue our path more firmly than before in order to take an effective step in achieving self-sufficiency in the manufacture of complex industrial parts.

Fardad Machine Paya Company is divided into the following departments with the aim of improving quality and accuracy in the process of preparing, compiling and developing products:

Product Engineering Department :

In this department, with the efforts of mechanical engineering, metallurgical engineering, and industrial engineering teams, we are trying to provide other units with a reliable process to guarantee the quality of the final product.

  • Mechanical Engineering Unit :
    • Measuring with precision measuring tools
    • Using 3D scanning devices with scanning accuracy of microns and hundredths
    • 3D modeling
    • 3D modeling assembly
    • Performing analyzes of three-dimensional models
    • Preparation of two-dimensional maps
  • Metallurgical unit :
    • Determining the material of parts
    • Determine the hardness of the material
    • Compilation of welding documents and WPS related parts
    • Compilation of documents for destructive and non-destructive DT and NDT tests
    • Compilation of heat treatment documents
    • Determining the type of parts coating
    • Determine the type of casting operation
  • Industrial Engineering Unit :
    • Compilation of the Gaunt Chart project schedule
    • Preparation of BOM parts
    • Preparation and compilation of technical encyclopedia Final Book
    • Presenting the project progress report to the employer
    • Extraction of standards and references used

Manufacturing Department :

In this department, we are trying to provide the most complex industrial parts with the most accurate manufacturing quality and the best and newest methods in the form of final products to our respected employers with the efforts of the manufacturing and production specialist team.

    • Compilation of technical knowledge of OPC production
    • Preparation of models and maps of each OPC stage
    • Ability to manufacture rotating parts with micron precision
    • Preparation of casting models and molds
    • Machining services with 4-axis, 5-axis CNC machines, boring, spark, carousel, all kinds of mills, gate mills, gun drill, honing, rolling, etc.
    • Performing all kinds of casting services including sand casting, centrifugal casting, precision casting
    • Performing various heat treatments
    • Carrying out coating processes including hard chrome, HVOF

The activities of other departments are summarized as follows :

  • Quality control department :
    • Determination of control criteria
    • Description of control processes
    • Compilation of control documents
    • Development of CP plan control chart
  • Support and Logistics Department :
    • Education
    • Support
    • Technical services

Assembly department :

The assembly department is one of the most important parts of production. After checking the desired products based on the assembly instructions and all assembly engineering drawings, the assembly process begins.
