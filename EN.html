About us :

Over the past years, we have proudly tried to be a designer, manufacturer and supplier of industrial equipment alongside the oil and gas, petrochemical, water and sewage, steel, aluminum, copper, etc. industries.

Therefore, due to the wideness of the needs of the mentioned industries, as well as taking steps towards the realization of the national mission and localizing the parts, relying on modern technical knowledge and engineering experiences, and the manufacture and knowledge of rotating and fixed parts and equipment such as pumps, turbines, compressors, etc. ..
