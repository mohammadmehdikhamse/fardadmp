About us :

We are a company in the field of design, reverse engineering, manufacturing, supplying and equipping all rotating and fixed industrial parts and equipment.

Fardad Machine Paya Industrial Group is trying to help the large and small industries by providing the best services in the field of engineering and construction.

Other activities of the company:

Production, commissioning and repairs of packaging machines used in the pharmaceutical, food, cosmetic and health industries with the relevant standards in the shortest time, the best quality and the lowest price.

Our products have a 12-month warranty and 24-hour and 12-year after-sales service. Also, periodic inspection of devices during the warranty period is one of our services.
