Products engineering Department :

In this department, with the efforts of mechanical engineering, metallurgical engineering, and industrial engineering teams, we are trying to provide other units with a reliable process to guarantee the quality of the final product.

  • Mechanical Engineering Unit :
    • Measuring with precision measuring tools
    • Using 3D scanning devices with scanning accuracy of microns and hundredths
    • 3D modeling
    • 3D modeling assembly
    • Performing analyzes of three-dimensional models
    • Preparation of two-dimensional maps
  • Metallurgical unit :
    • Determining the material of parts
    • Determine the hardness of the material
    • Compilation of welding documents and WPS related parts
    • Compilation of documents for destructive and non-destructive DT and NDT tests
    • Compilation of heat treatment documents
    • Determining the type of parts coating
    • Determine the type of casting operation
  • Industrial Engineering Unit :
    • Compilation of the Gaunt Chart project schedule
    • Preparation of BOM parts
    • Preparation and compilation of technical encyclopedia Final Book
    • Presenting the project progress report to the employer
    • Extraction of standards and references used
