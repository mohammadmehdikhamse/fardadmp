Automatic shrink sleeve machine :

About Machine :

The sleeve applicator machines are designed for covering full bottlesbody or partial labeling and it has two label cutting and pulling units which can be adjusted by servo motors which are calibrated in milimeter too .

The machine base frame is completely made of stainless steel and all parts are easily replaceable.

The machine is suitable for pharmaceutical industries,food industries, hygiene industries, etc.

1 Labeling speed 100 - 200 pcs/min
2 Labeling accuracy ±1 mm
3 Applicable product diameter D : 40 - 100 mm | H : 100 - 300 mm
4 Voltage 220v - 50hz single phase
5 Power 2500w
6 Machine weight 480 kg
7 Machine size 1100 × 2500 × 2000 mm ( W×L×H )
8 Touch panel control DELTA
9 Rolling drive servo motor
10 Material Stainless steel
11 Sensor Micro detector & selet
